## Phase 2 — what exists (server/)
- Portal served by the same Render service as the old API (`/` portal, `/api/*` old tracker API unchanged).
- Sign-in with username or email; scrypt passwords; server-side sessions in Postgres (httpOnly cookie, 12 h absolute, 2 h idle); CSRF token on every change plus same-host check; rate limits; lock after 10 failures for 15 min.
- Two-step verification (TOTP + 8 single-use recovery codes, seeds encrypted at rest); mandatory for Super Admin.
- Password recovery by single-use 30-minute link (email via outbox); temporary passwords force a change at next sign-in.
- Roles: Super Admin, Project Manager, Finance, Engineer, Viewer, Brokerage Agent, Sales Manager, External viewer; section abilities editable in Administration › Roles.
- Workspaces for all four sections (Abuquwa Villas migrated as DEV-2026-001), references per section and year, portfolio pages with search, status filters, sorting and paging; overview/edit, archive by status.
- Membership-based isolation: non-members get 404 for pages, files and thumbnails; Super Admin sees all. External viewers: My Workspaces, view-only, published files only, every write refused by middleware.
- Documents: multi-file upload (25 MB each), content-type sniffing, thumbnails, caption/location/observation date, internal vs published, protected download.
- Append-only audit log (database trigger refuses edits/deletes); per-workspace activity; Administration: users, roles, outbox, audit log, system.
- Outbox: every email recorded; status "Not configured" until SMTP variables are set; password-reset contents never displayed.

## Phase 3 — what exists
- Guided setup (8 steps + review) with save/resume, "Not yet determined" per question, validation, missing-data and conflict checks; stage answers update the workspace stage.
- Draft plan generator (deterministic, no AI): phases, tasks, milestones by project type/stage/outcome; suggested risks, approvals tracker item, stakeholders, budget lines without invented amounts. Suggested items are labelled; onboarding statuses are marked "reported, unverified".
- Plan approval by the workspace manager: requires phase weights and review confirmation; saves a baseline snapshot (phases, tasks, dates, weights, budget); revisions need a reason; versions kept.
- Tasks & Schedule: phases with weights, tasks with owners, dates, actuals, sub-tasks, milestones, dependencies (loop check), overdue flags, comments; contributors may only update status/progress/actuals.

- Budget & Cost (BHD minor units): lines (baseline, current, forecast uncommitted), commitments, supplier invoices (submit → approve/reject, duplicate numbers refused), payments (recorded → confirmed by Finance; reversal with reason; over-payment refused). Committed / incurred / paid / open commitments / EAC kept distinct.
- Progress updates with multiple photos, captions, work area, observation date, draft/publish, feed + gallery filters, comments; photos never change progress; optional explicit "apply % to task".
- Registers: risks, issues, decisions, changes, permits & approvals, stakeholders, procurement, quality, meetings, handover & defects.
- Units & Sales (list price, status); reports: PDF (branded, with photos, period filter) and Excel (separate sheets, formula-injection safe, finance sheets only for finance-visible roles).

## Agreements & contracts — what exists

- Letterhead: A4 PNG/JPG placed behind every page of generated PDFs and Word files; header/footer space configurable.
- 12 starter drafts (subcontract, design consultancy, unit sale, project management services, residential lease, commercial lease, property management, maintenance contract, sale mandate, buyer search agreement, reservation form, co-broker). Generic texts, not checked against any jurisdiction; every page they generate carries a legal-review notice. "Make a company copy" to edit wording (versioned, no notice).

- Preparing an agreement: system fills project/property and company details and the reference; asks for the other party (existing or new; updates the party record) and the template's terms; save with blanks, generate only when required answers are complete; blanks print as lines for hand completion, never invented.
- Every generation is a numbered version (PDF + Word for text templates); issue for signature; record signing date + signed copy (PDF/photo); signed/cancelled agreements are locked; all steps audited.

## Phase 6 — Property Sales & Acquisition (what exists)
- Section dashboard with tabs: Sales Listings, Acquisition Requests, Prospective Buyers, Viewings, Offers & Deals, Fees & Reports; buttons Add Property for Sale, Create Property Search Request, Add Prospective Buyer. Lists show stage, mandate, price/budget, open enquiries and next follow-up.
- Mandates (seller agreement for a listing, acquisition agreement for a request): versioned; scope, exclusivity, dates, renewal, price authority / purchasing constraints, marketing scope and budget, fee (fixed or percentage with an explicit base — nothing assumed), payer, earning trigger, VAT, payment days, documented dual representation and co-broker split, client decisions, signed agreement link. Activity (marketing, viewings, offers) is blocked until the mandate is active and the authority to represent the client is verified, and again when it expires or is suspended. An expired mandate is not a completed sale.
- Property file: public fields (shown on the brochure) separate from confidential ones (minimum acceptable price, title, access, restrictions, source). Every asking-price change keeps date, reason and seller authorisation. Listing status (draft → awaiting verification → ready → available → under offer / reserved → sold, withdrawn, archived) is separate from mandate status; "available" needs verified information, a price and an active verified mandate; under offer / reserved / sold follow the recorded offer and deal and are never set by hand. Photos and documents tagged client-approved or internal. Optional cross-link to a managed unit without changing its lease. SarayDar-branded brochure PDF with approved photos only.
- Marketing log: channel, reference, link, costs against the approved budget; "published" only with evidence. The system does not post anywhere.
- Prospective buyers: register with contact, source, agent, criteria (unknown kept as unknown), status separate from each property enquiry; per-property enquiries with stages, next action and follow-up (required, or a recorded reason), lost reasons; communication log; overdue and stale (30 days) views; duplicate detection on the same email or phone only, with a merge that keeps all history; conversion of a prospect into an acquisition request without losing history.
- Acquisition requests: versioned brief (changes need a reason and evidence), status, external opportunities (source, verification, date checked, availability, documented arrangement — never treated as a SarayDar listing), transparent matching (each criterion matched / not / unknown, no score, no currency conversion), shortlist with ranking, reasons, internal notes, recorded client feedback, rejection reasons; publishing to the client requires an availability and price recheck within 14 days. The seller's confidential minimum never appears.
- Viewings: linked to property, buyer, request and agent; Bahrain time; overlap warning for the same property or agent; tentative / confirmed / completed / cancelled / rescheduled / no-show; completed viewings need feedback; owner summary kept separate from private buyer data; past viewings without an outcome are flagged.
- Offers: every version kept (buyer offer, seller counter…); decisions recorded from outside the portal with who decided, date and evidence, against the exact version; asking, offered, accepted and final prices kept apart; acceptance moves the listing to "under offer", never to sold.
- Deals: workspace opened from an accepted offer with the exact interest being sold; one live or completed deal per listing (database rule); reservation only with authorisation and recorded deposit holder; editable due-diligence/closing checklist with owners, dates, dependencies and evidence; completion needs the checklist closed, the final price and completion evidence — then the listing is sold, the buyer's enquiry won, other enquiries lost with reason, the request completed; fall-through keeps history and re-opens the listing.
- Fees: commission invoice drafts from each side's own mandate (percentage of the recorded base with the calculation shown and rounding stated, or fixed), at most one per deal and side (database rule); issued, paid, receipted and statemented on the shared fee ledger. Gross deal value is never SarayDar revenue. SarayDar does not hold client money (no client-money ledger, since none is authorised).
- Sales expenses: SarayDar cost vs client-reimbursable vs paid by client directly, approval, payment, against the marketing budget.
- Reports (period, only cases the user can open): listings by status, days on market (definition shown), mandates ending, marketing, enquiries by source vs unique buyers, viewings and offers by status, lost reasons and fallen-through deals, completed sales value (not revenue), pipeline (forecast, not receipts), fees issued/collected/outstanding, expenses by payer; PDF/Excel.
- Not done in Phase 6: seller/buyer viewer portals (Phase 7), Arabic descriptions (interface is English by decision), configurable pipeline stages, AI suggestions, multi-currency conversion.

## Phase 5 — Clients Properties Management (what exists)
- Owner → Property → Unit hierarchy; one owner with several properties; co-owners with recorded shares and statement rule (nothing split automatically). Property Setup records who collects rent, holds deposits, pays suppliers, owner transfers, fee deduction permission, maintenance limit per job, emergency rule and limit, reserve, billing entity (owner or SarayDar) and payment instructions printed on invoices.
- Units: single or numbered range; floor, type, area, bedrooms, features, condition, market rent; statuses available / reserved / occupied / under maintenance / unavailable with dated history.
- Leases and tenants: draft → activate (database rule refuses overlapping effective leases on a unit; renewals start after the previous end), rent changes from a date, recurring charges, billing frequency 1/3/6/12 months, part-month proration, due day, deposit, occupants, link to the signed lease from Agreements; move-in/out, inspections, keys, meters with photos; early termination; renewal drafts; expiry warnings (60 days).
- Opening balances (tenant arrears, deposits held, owner cash at takeover): recorded with evidence → approved by Finance → posted once; duplicates refused.
- Rent ledger: charges come from lease terms; invoices are built from charges (RNT-YYYY-NNNNN), one per lease period — retries, concurrent runs and resends never duplicate a charge or invoice (unique indexes + row lock + advisory lock).
- Automatic invoicing (required now): Finance switches it on per lease (first period, days before the period, delivery, reminder days). A background job runs hourly (Bahrain time): issues due invoices, emails the PDF to the tenant, sends reminders once per configured day after the due date, records every attempt in the Outbox with its real status (sent / failed / email not configured — never falsely sent). Resend reuses the same invoice. Manual "Run now" for Finance. Without Outlook SMTP, invoices are still generated and kept with status "Email not configured". Note: on Render's free tier the service sleeps when idle, so the hourly job runs only while it is awake — the paid tier is needed for reliable automatic invoicing.
- Tenant payments: reported (incl. uncleared cheques) → confirmed by Finance (cleared date) → allocated oldest-first; receipts (RRC-…); bounce/reversal restores the balance and keeps history; duplicate reference and repeated submission refused; credit notes on a charge never above its balance; tenant statement (page + PDF) with opening/closing balance. Viewing or sending an invoice never marks it paid. No payment gateway and no Pay Now button in this release; the ledger (charges, allocations, idempotency keys) is ready for a gateway later.
- Deposits: separate ledger (received / approved deduction with reason + approval / refund, never more than held); not rent and not owner funds until an approved deduction.
- Maintenance: requests by staff (including tenant/owner requests received by phone or email) for a unit or common area, with before photos; provider, target, estimate; approval within the owner's limit, owner approval recorded with reference, or configured emergency rule with justification (audited); work cannot start before approval; completion with after photos; verification; cost responsibility with recorded basis; warranty date; full history.
- Expenses: recorded → approved (maintenance invoices only after the work was approved) → payment confirmed by Finance; who pays (from held funds, owner directly, tenant, SarayDar); shared building costs split equally or by area into exact unit shares (never the full amount per unit).
- Cleaning & services: recurring schedules (weekly, fortnightly, monthly, quarterly) with checklist, provider, cost; tasks generated 60 days ahead without duplicates; done/missed with photos; supervisor review; overdue flags.
- Owner Account: statement for any period — rent received by SarayDar, approved deposit deductions, contributions, expenses paid from held funds, authorised fee deductions, recorded transfers; cash held vs available to transfer (after unpaid approved expenses and the reserve); tenant arrears and deposits shown separately; rent collected directly by the owner shown as information. Transfers above the available amount are refused. Fee deduction (only if the owner allowed it) settles the fee invoice in Fee Billing as "Deduction from rent" and appears once on the statement. PDF statement.
- Fee Billing for properties uses the same ledger as client projects, addressed to the owner.
- Unit History & Report (mandatory): any unit(s) or whole building, presets (month, last month, quarter, year, all) or custom dates; tenancies overlapping the period incl. earlier starts, vacant periods, moves, agreed rent steps; maintenance with dates, provider, approvals, responsibility and before/after photos, open at start / unresolved at end; unit cleaning; expenses with unit share and basis; incurred vs paid vs unpaid at end; rent due, collected, outstanding at start and end (as-of policy documented); chronological history; PDF with photos and Excel workbook (Summary, Tenancy, Maintenance, Finance).
- Dashboard and reports: occupancy (denominator disclosed: excludes unavailable units, includes units under maintenance), vacant units, rent due/collected this month, arrears, leases ending in 60 days, open maintenance, overdue service tasks, unpaid fees; reports for rent roll, occupancy over a period, aged arrears, lease expiry, maintenance, expenses, fee collection — all exportable to PDF/Excel.
- Not done in Phase 5: owner and tenant portals (Phase 7), tenant self-service requests, enquiries/viewings for vacant units (shares the Phase 6 CRM), payment gateway, combined fee agreements covering several properties (fees are per property).

## Phase 4 — Clients Projects Management (what exists)
- Client register (`/clients`): individual/company, main contact, authorised representatives, phone, email, address, preferred contact, billing details, tax number, reference, approval authorities, communication responsibilities; linked projects, agreements, correspondence log, fees outstanding, statement across the client's projects. One client can have several projects.
- Client projects use the full project engine (setup, plots, plan/baseline, tasks, registers, progress updates with photos, agreements, documents, reports, exports). The section list shows the client name first, progress, next milestone and unpaid fees.
- Guided onboarding for client projects: project & objectives, site/designs/approvals, current stage, client budget, timeline, contractors/consultants/team, risks. The draft plan uses client-project phases (onboarding, design coordination, approvals, procurement & appointments, construction supervision, handover, defects). The client's approved total is kept as one "unallocated" budget line until split.
- Client & Agreement tab: management-agreement terms as versions (reference, dates, scope, responsibilities, exclusions, monthly fee, billing in advance/arrears, part-month rule, payment days, tax treatment, client approvers). Drafted by the project manager, activated by Finance; old versions kept; a fee change applies from its effective date and cannot rewrite periods already invoiced.
- Fee Billing (SarayDar's own ledger, separate tables from the client's construction spending): monthly draft per month (once per month, calculated day by day from the terms in force, prorated if agreed), authorised extra lines, issue by Finance (INV-YYYY-NNNN, due date from terms), void with reason (only without payments/credits), credit notes (CN-…, never above the outstanding amount), confirmed client payments (duplicate bank reference and repeated form submission refused), allocation across invoices (never above payment or invoice balance), unapplied credit, receipts as a separate step (RCT-…), payment and allocation reversals that keep history, statement with opening/closing balance and period filter, overdue alerts on the home page. PDFs: invoice / tax invoice, receipt, credit note, statement, on the letterhead when uploaded.
- VAT: owner decision 2026-10-23 — no VAT for now. New terms default to "No VAT charged". When VAT starts, record a new terms version from that date with the rate; earlier invoices stay as issued. A VAT invoice cannot be issued until SarayDar's VAT registration number is entered (Admin › Agreements › company details); it then prints on the tax invoice.
- Client Budget & Payments: commitments (contract, order, consultant appointment, approved variation), contractor payment applications / consultant fees / invoices with submitted → reviewed → approved steps (client projects must be reviewed before approval), payments recorded with who paid (client direct, SarayDar on the client's behalf with a client-approval reference, other) and confirmed only by Finance. None of it is counted as SarayDar revenue or cost.
- Client Approvals: requests and externally obtained decisions (budget, appointment, material, variation, payment, design) with approver, date, method, notes, evidence files and linked version; decisions are final (a change needs a new request); "client can see status" flag ready for the Phase 7 portal.
- Fixed with this phase: calendar dates are now read from the database as plain dates, so they no longer shift by a day when the server clock is not UTC.
- Not done in Phase 4: automatic monthly issuing (terms record the flag, nothing issues automatically — needs the paid tier/job scheduler), emailing invoices (needs Outlook SMTP), configurable approval thresholds, the external client view (Phase 7).
