# SarayDar Management System — user guide

## Getting around
- After you sign in, **Home** shows the four sections. It also shows "Needs your attention" and recent activity.
- Open a section to see its portfolio list, then open a workspace (a project, property, listing or request). Its tabs are along the top.
- On every page, the buttons at the top right are **Back**, **Home**, **PDF** and **Excel**. PDF and Excel export the page exactly as you see it.
- Each workspace has an **Assistant** tab. It works only when the AI service is configured, and it only gives suggestions. It never changes records.

## SarayDar Real Estate Development
1. Go to **Create New Project**. Fill in the guided setup. Any question can be answered "Not yet determined".
2. Review the draft plan. The manager approves it, and that saves the baseline.
3. Day to day:
   - Update tasks and actual dates in **Tasks & Schedule**.
   - Record costs in **Budget & Cost**: commitment → invoice → approval → payment confirmed by Finance.
4. **Progress Updates**:
   - Add photos, work done and next steps, then Publish.
   - To let the client or investors see an update, tick **show to external viewers**.
   - Issues, notes and workforce always stay internal.
5. **Investors & Funding** (Finance and managers):
   - Record each investor's participation and agreement.
   - Record transfers the investor reports. Finance confirms them, and that issues the acknowledgment.
   - Record returns and distributions with their approval.
   - The statement can be filtered by dates and saved as PDF or Excel.

## Clients Projects Management
- **Client & Agreement:** record the management terms. Finance activates them.
- **Fee Billing:** create the monthly draft, Finance issues it, then record and allocate payments and issue receipts. The statement is under the same tab.
- **Client Budget & Payments:** record the client's own construction spending. It is never counted as SarayDar money.
- **Client Approvals:** record decisions that reached you outside the portal, with evidence. Tick "client can see status" to show it in their portal.

## Clients Properties Management
- **Setup** sets the owner's rules: who collects rent, the maintenance limit and the reserve.
- **Units → leases → automatic rent invoicing.** Finance switches invoicing on for each lease.
- **Payments:** a payment is reported, then Finance confirms it. Receipts, credits and deposits are recorded the same way.
- **Maintenance:**
  1. Record the request with "before" photos.
  2. Get approval, either within the owner's limit or with a recorded owner approval.
  3. Complete it with "after" photos and verify the work.
  4. Use **Show to owner** to share the job in the owner's portal.
- **Owner Account** shows the statement and the amount available to transfer. **Unit History & Report** shows the full history of any apartment over any period.

## Property Sales & Acquisition
- **Seller side.** Activate and verify the mandate first. Then:
  - Fill in the property file. Its public and confidential fields are kept separate.
  - Set the price, which needs the seller's authorisation.
  - Log marketing with evidence, and record viewings with an owner summary.
  - Record offers: each version is kept, and you record the decision made outside the portal.
  - Run the deal checklist, then complete the sale with evidence.
- **Buyer side.** Record the acquisition mandate and the brief (versioned). Then:
  - Use matching, which marks each criterion as met, not met or unknown.
  - Add options to the shortlist. Publishing an item to the client needs a recheck within 14 days.
  - Record viewings and the buyer's feedback, then offers.
- **Prospective buyers.** Every enquiry needs a next follow-up date or a reason. Watch the overdue and stale lists.
- **Fees.** The commission invoice is drafted from the mandate, then issued, paid and receipted.

## For clients, owners, investors, sellers and buyers (external viewers)
1. Open the invitation link from SarayDar and choose a password. The link works once and expires after 7 days.
2. **My Workspaces** lists only the projects, properties and cases shared with you, grouped as Projects, Properties and Sales & Acquisitions. Use the filter box to find one.
3. Open one to see its published progress and photos (tap a photo for full size), schedule, documents, and anything else SarayDar has shared with you, such as your statement.
4. The portal is **view-only**. To approve something, ask a question or send a document, contact SarayDar directly. Staff record your decision in the system.
5. PDF/Excel export and file downloads appear only if SarayDar enabled them for you.
6. To change your password or sign out, use the round button with your initial at the top right, then **Account**. If you forget your password, use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page.

The portal works on mobile phones. No app is needed: use the phone's browser.
